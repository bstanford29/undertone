What Undertone looks like

Seven surfaces, in the order you meet them. The first one is a working animation of the hold-to-talk loop. Everything follows the Mac's light or dark appearance, so flip this page's theme to see both.

1. The pill

This is 95% of the app. You never open a window. Hold the key, a pill rises from the bottom of the screen with a live level meter. Let go and it turns into a spinner for under a second, then a check with the round-trip time, then it drops away. Press play to run the loop.

Sounds: a short low tick on key down, a higher tick on insert. Both off by a setting. The pill never takes focus, so your caret stays where it was.

2. Command Mode

Select text first, then hold the key and say what to do with it. The pill changes its label so you know it will rewrite instead of insert. The selection is replaced in place.

Mail
Hi Priya, I wanted to reach out and touch base regarding the sprint plan that we walked through yesterday because I believe the version that was shared might not be the most recent one that we have on our side.
Rewrite selection · "make this shorter"

3. Menu bar

The only permanent presence. One icon. The menu shows whether the models are warm, the cleanup level, and the things you asked for: last transcript back, undo the AI edit, history, dictionary, meetings.

5. Dictionary

Two lists and an inbox. Terms are words whisper should spell right. Replacements turn a spoken phrase into stored text, which is also how snippets work. The inbox is where learned words wait for a yes.

6. Meeting notes

Start from the menu or the hotkey. No bot joins. The Mac's own audio and your mic are recorded as two channels, which gives "Me" and "Others" for free. Transcript grows live. End it and a summary lands in Obsidian.
